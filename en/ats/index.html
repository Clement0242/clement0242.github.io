        Under the hood no LLM: classic NLP, readable end to end

        1. Read

        The job ad is split into sentences, lower-cased, stripped of accents and stop words. Headings matter: whatever follows “Nice to have” or “Bonus” weighs half as much as what is required.

        2. Spot

        A lexicon of some forty skills, each with its French and English synonyms and my real level, from “no” to “every day”. What I don't have becomes a minus, in black and white.

        3. Compare

        TF-IDF over the job ad and every line of my background, then cosine similarity: the machine finds the line of my CV closest to your need, even when the keywords differ.

        4. Score

        A logistic regression combines skill coverage, similarity, domain (sport or not) and the experience gap. The weights? Hand-calibrated on a handful of reference job ads. Yes: artisanal overfitting. A real model would need labelled job ads — recruiters, you know where to find me.
